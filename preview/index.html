About Us

Our Mission

To develop young people through a balanced programme of spiritual, physical, mental, and social activities, helping them become responsible citizens and leaders in the church and community.

Our Vision

To be a leading Christian youth organisation that shapes the next generation of leaders who are grounded in faith, discipline, and service to humanity.

Spiritual Growth

Building strong Christian foundations through Bible study, prayer, and worship.

Physical Development

Encouraging fitness through drill, sports, and outdoor activities.

Mental Training

Developing knowledge and skills through badge work and training programmes.
